# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People planning weddings, receptions, family celebrations, and corporate gatherings in and around Gachibowli who need to quickly judge whether SR Conventions fits their event and start an enquiry.

## Product Purpose

Present SR Conventions as a credible local event venue and turn visitor interest into direct enquiries. Success means a visitor can understand the venue, explore representative event settings, locate it, and begin a WhatsApp enquiry from a phone without searching through third-party directories.

## Positioning

The site gives this specific Gachibowli venue a direct, venue-owned presentation instead of making prospective customers piece together photos and facts from marketplace listings.

## Operating Context

Most visitors will arrive on mobile while comparing venues. They need a fast visual read, examples of suitable event types, practical location information, and a low-friction enquiry path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-page responsive marketing website.
- Primary conversion action is WhatsApp.
- The demo must use a clearly marked placeholder WhatsApp number until the venue confirms its contact details.
- No backend or live availability system is included.
- Do not invent capacity, prices, awards, customer testimonials, or package claims.
- Publicly verified location: Plot Nos. 13, 14, 20 and 21, Sri Sai Housing Colony, Gowlidoddi, Q City Road, near Hyundai showroom, Gachibowli, Hyderabad, Telangana 500032.

## Evidence on Hand

- Google Maps shows a substantial operating presence with hundreds of reviews and photos.
- Public directory listings identify the venue as an indoor banquet facility used for weddings, receptions, and gatherings.
- No approved logo, brand guide, venue-owned photography, package information, confirmed phone number, or testimonials have been provided.
- Demo photography must be clearly identified as representative placeholder imagery and replaced before production use.

## Product Principles

- Make the venue desirable without making unsupported claims.
- Put enquiry within one tap throughout the mobile journey.
- Let event imagery carry the emotional case while practical details remain easy to scan.
- Be transparent about placeholder content in the pitch demo.
- Treat location and direct contact as first-class conversion information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-visible focus, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, descriptive image alternatives, and responsive layouts suitable for mobile visitors.
